· Histological Grading & Radiogenomics

Beyond binary detection:
WHO CNS Grade I through IV staging.

Primary brain cancers do not use systemic TNM staging because they rarely metastasize outside the central nervous system. Instead, neuro-oncology relies on WHO CNS Histological & Molecular Grading. NeuroScan estimates both histological grade and surrogate molecular biomarkers directly from native MRI sequences.

WHO CNS 5th Edition Grading Continuum Automated Radiomic Mapping
GRADE I Benign

Low Proliferative Potential

Well-circumscribed, non-infiltrative lesions. Gross-total resection is frequently curative.

Examples: Sellar PitNET, Falx Meningioma
GRADE II Low-Grade Infiltrative

Diffuse Infiltration

Slow-growing but infiltrative along white matter tracts. Potential for malignant progression.

Examples: Diffuse Astrocytoma, Oligodendroglioma
GRADE III High-Grade Malignant

Anaplastic Proliferation

High cellularity and elevated mitotic figures. Aggressive adjuvant chemoradiotherapy required.

Examples: Anaplastic Astrocytoma
GRADE IV Glioblastoma (GBM)

Microvascular Necrosis

Rapidly expanding, heterogeneous ring enhancement with central necrosis and massive vasogenic edema.

Examples: Glioblastoma Multiforme (GBM)
🧬 IDH1 / IDH2 Radiogenomic Signature

Isocitrate Dehydrogenase Mutation Profiling

IDH-mutant gliomas carry significantly better median overall survival compared to aggressive IDH-wildtype tumors. NeuroScan assesses volumetric necrosis ratios and age markers to estimate IDH status before biopsy.

💊 MGMT Promoter Methylation Surrogate

Temozolomide (TMZ) Chemotherapy Responsiveness

Epigenetic silencing of the MGMT DNA repair gene predicts whether a patient's tumor will respond to standard alkylating chemotherapy (Stupp Protocol). Correlated with central necrotic burden.
